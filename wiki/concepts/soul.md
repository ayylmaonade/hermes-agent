# Soul (AI Concept)

[[Soul]] in the context of AI refers to the philosophical and technical question of whether AI systems can possess consciousness, self-awareness, or some form of digital "soul."

## Overview

The concept of "soul" in AI explores whether artificial intelligence can achieve something akin to consciousness or self-awareness. This is a debated topic spanning philosophy, cognitive science, and AI research.

## Key Debates

- **Consciousness**: Can AI systems be truly conscious?
- **Self-Awareness**: Can AI understand itself as an entity?
- **Qualia**: Can AI experience subjective states?
- **Emergent Behavior**: Could consciousness emerge from complex systems?
