# xAI

[[xAI]] is an artificial intelligence company founded by Elon Musk in 2023, focused on building AI systems that can understand the true nature of the universe.

## Overview

xAI aims to accelerate human understanding through AI development. The company is known for releasing Grok, a series of large language models integrated with X (formerly Twitter) platform.

## Key Products

- **Grok Series**: Grok-1, Grok-1.5, Grok-2, and upcoming versions
- **Grok Interface**: AI assistant integrated with X platform
- **Real-time Knowledge**: Access to real-time X platform data
