# Anthropic

[[Anthropic]] is an AI safety company focused on building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. Founded in 2021 by former members of OpenAI's safety team.

## Overview

Anthropic is dedicated to studying AI systems' capabilities, behaviors, and safety. The company develops large language models with a focus on AI safety and alignment research.

## Key Products

- **Claude Series**: Family of AI assistants including Claude 2, Claude 2.1, and Claude 3
- **Constitutional AI**: Research on AI safety and alignment
- **Model Outputs**: AI responses for various applications
