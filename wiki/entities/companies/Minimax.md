# Minimax

[[Minimax]] is a Chinese AI company founded in 2021, known for developing large language models and AI voice technologies.

## Overview

Minimax is an independent AI company based in Shanghai, China. The company focuses on building foundational AI models and applications for various use cases including customer service, content creation, and conversational AI.

## Key Products

- **Abab Series**: Large language model family
- **AI Voice**: Text-to-speech and voice synthesis technology
- **MiniMax API**: Developer API for AI models
