# 01 AI

[[01-ai]] is a leading AI company focused on developing large language models and AI systems. Founded in 2023, the company is based in Beijing, China.

## Overview

01 AI (One AI) is a Chinese artificial intelligence company that specializes in developing open-source large language models. The company is known for creating the **ChatGLM** series of models, which have gained significant attention in the open-source AI community.

## Key Products

- **ChatGLM Series**: A family of efficient large language models
- **Open-Source Models**: Commitment to making AI research accessible
