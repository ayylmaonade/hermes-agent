# Meta

[[Meta]] (formerly Facebook) is a technology company with significant investments in AI research and development through Meta AI.

## Overview

Meta AI is Meta Platforms' artificial intelligence research laboratory and development division. The company is known for open-sourcing significant AI models including the LLaMA series.

## Key Products

- **LLaMA Series**: Open-source large language models (LLaMA, LLaMA2, LLaMA3)
- **Llama.cpp**: Optimized inference library (in collaboration with community)
- **PyTorch**: Deep learning framework (acquired from Meta AI Research)
- **Code Llama**: Code-specific language models
