# OpenAI

[[OpenAI]] is an AI research laboratory consisting of the for-profit corporation OpenAI LP and its parent company, the non-profit OpenAI Inc. Founded in 2015, it has become one of the most prominent AI companies.

## Overview

OpenAI has been a leader in developing large language models and AI systems. The company gained widespread attention with the release of GPT-3, GPT-3.5, and the GPT-4 series.

## Key Products

- **GPT Series**: GPT-3, GPT-3.5 (Turbo), GPT-4, GPT-4 Turbo
- **ChatGPT**: Conversational AI interface
- **DALL-E**: Text-to-image generation models
- **Whisper**: Speech recognition model
- **API Platform**: Developer API for AI models
