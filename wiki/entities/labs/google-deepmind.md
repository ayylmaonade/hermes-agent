# Google DeepMind

[[google-deepmind]] is a leading AI research laboratory and subsidiary of Alphabet Inc., formed from the merger of Google AI and DeepMind in 2023.

## Overview

DeepMind was founded in 2010 in London and was acquired by Google in 2014. The merger with Google AI created Google DeepMind, one of the world's most advanced AI research organizations.

## Key Achievements

- **AlphaGo**: First AI to beat a world champion Go player
- **AlphaFold**: Protein structure prediction system
- **Gemini**: Multi-modal AI model family
- **Mind's Eye**: Visual reasoning research
- **Gato**: General-purpose AI agent
