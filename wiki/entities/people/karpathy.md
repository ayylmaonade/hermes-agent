# Andrej Karpathy

[[karpathy]] is a prominent AI researcher and educator known for his work at Tesla, OpenAI, and his educational content on deep learning.

## Overview

Andrej Karpathy is a computer scientist who has made significant contributions to deep learning and AI. He served as the Director of AI at Tesla and was a key member of the OpenAI team. He is well-known for his accessible educational content on YouTube and his blog.

## Key Contributions

- **Deep Learning Education**: Created popular YouTube tutorials and blog posts
- **Tesla AI**: Led Autopilot and vision teams
- **OpenAI**: Early team member, contributed to GPT development
- **CodeCarGenius**: Research on code generation
